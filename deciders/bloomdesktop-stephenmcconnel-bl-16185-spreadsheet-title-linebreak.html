What this PR is about

Problem

A book whose title is two paragraphs (e.g. “- 1 -” / “Beginning with God”) exports to a spreadsheet as two lines in one cell. After another program saved the spreadsheet and it was imported back, the title came back with literal _x000d_ text in place of the line break.

Cause

Spreadsheet files escape special characters as _xHHHH_. Excel writes the hex digits in uppercase, but the standard doesn't require that, and the other program wrote the carriage return as _x000d_. EPPlus and Bloom's own decoder both recognized only uppercase, so the lowercase escape survived as text.

What the PR does

  • Escapes are decoded whatever the case of their hex digits, in one pass. The x must still be lowercase, so text like _X000D_ is left alone.
  • A carriage return on its own now starts a new paragraph on import, as a line feed or CR+LF already did.
  • A CR+LF split across two formatted parts of a cell still makes exactly one break, and a further line feed after it still makes its blank paragraph.
  • Tests: an export → re-save → import round trip of a two-paragraph title, plus decoder and paragraph-building cases.
