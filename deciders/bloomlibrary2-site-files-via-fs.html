What this PR is about

Depends on bloom-core-supabase #15 (the worker accepts ?v= and caches versioned URLs for a year) being deployed to the production worker first. Until then ?v= is ignored: the files are served, just not cached.

Purpose

The site's book downloads (PDF, ePUB, BloomPUB/.bloomd, .bloomSource) now come through api.bloomlibrary.org/v1/fs instead of straight from S3, with a version in the URL. Direct S3 downloads are billed AWS egress and invisible to Cloudflare's rules, and S3 URLs can show the uploader's email. The version lets the worker cache every download for a year without ever serving a stale file.

What the PR does

  • A new FsUrl.ts builds /v1/fs/{bucket}/{bookId}/{path}?v={epochMs}: upload for the PDF, harvest for the rest, with a dev- prefix for sandbox books.
  • The PDF is versioned by lastUploaded; harvested files by harvestStartedAt, offered only once the harvest is Done. No usable version gives the unversioned URL.
  • Names are encoded for the fs function's double decode: a literal + or % is double-encoded, everything else once.
  • No S3 fallback: a download whose fs URL can't be built isn't offered, and its button is disabled or hidden.
  • Download buttons take the file name from the URL without its ?v=. Reading online and shellbooks are unchanged.

Earlier work on reading online through fs is kept on site-files-via-fs-versioned.
