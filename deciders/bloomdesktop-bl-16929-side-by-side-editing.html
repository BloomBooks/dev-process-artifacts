What this PR is about

Purpose

Bloom's Edit tab shows one page at a time. People laying out a children's book want to see the page they are editing beside its facing page, and the rest of the book around it, the way it will be read. This PR adds that view, and makes changing pages hold steady on screen in both views.

What the PR does

  • A page view chooser under the account avatar switches between one page and all pages, remembered across pages and sessions; choosing all pages zooms out if the spread would not fit.
  • All pages lays the book out as it is read: cover alone, then facing pages, with a blank page before the back cover when the printed book needs one. Right-to-left books are mirrored, calendars stack, screen-sized books show single pages, and corner marks show the page being edited. The other pages are read-only, sandboxed, drawn only near the screen, and follow Format dialog changes. None of it is saved.
  • Clicking another page opens it in the same place on screen and replays the click (caret in text, picture selected).
  • Page changes hold steady in both views: the new page loads under the old one and replaces it when laid out. This relies on BL-13502's saving without a reload, now on master.
  • Remove Page marks the page being edited with a red X while the confirmation is up.
  • The controls above the page (Change Layout, game tabs) sit in a bar fixed across the top of the page frame.
