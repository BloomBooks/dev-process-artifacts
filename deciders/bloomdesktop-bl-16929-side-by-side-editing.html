What this PR is about

Purpose

Bloom's Edit tab shows one page at a time. People laying out a children's book want to see the page they are editing beside its facing page, and the rest of the book around it, the way it will be read. This PR adds that view.

What the PR does

  • A page view chooser under the account avatar switches between one page and all pages. The choice and each view's zoom are kept as a Bloom user setting, so they last from page to page and after Bloom is closed. Choosing all pages zooms out further whenever the spread would not fit at the zoom kept for it.
  • All pages lays the book out as it is read: cover alone, then facing pages, with a blank page before the back cover when the printed book needs one. Right-to-left books are mirrored, calendars stack, screen-sized books show single pages, and purple corner marks show the page being edited. The other pages are read-only, sandboxed, drawn only near the screen, and follow Format dialog changes. None of it is saved.
  • Clicking another page opens it in the same place on screen and replays the click (caret in text, picture selected).
  • Page changes: with one page, the new page shows in place as soon as it is drawn. With all pages, the corner marks move to the clicked page at once, and the old page stays, under a wait cursor, until the new one and its neighbours are drawn. The user can type into the new page as soon as a text box has the focus: master's BL-17022 keeps text typed while CKEditor loads.
  • The book's page layout is brought up to date at once. BL-16893 runs its page layout update only when the AI image editor or a Publish tool needs it, but All Pages shows every other page as last saved. So choosing All Pages, or showing a page with it on (after a page size or Appearance change, or on opening such a book), runs the update if the book needs it and comes back to the page. A book whose update fails is not tried again from All Pages until its layout changes again.
  • Remove Page marks the page being edited with a red X while the confirmation is up, and the controls above the page sit in a bar fixed across the top of the page frame.
