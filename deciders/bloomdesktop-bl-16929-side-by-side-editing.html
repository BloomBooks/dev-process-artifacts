What this PR is about

Purpose

Bloom's Edit tab shows one page at a time. People laying out a children's book want to see the page they are editing beside its facing page, and the rest of the book around it, the way it will be read. This PR adds that view. It also keeps the user out of a page until it is ready to use, since text typed into a page that is still starting up is lost; on an old, slow machine that takes several seconds.

What the PR does

  • A page view chooser under the account avatar switches between one page and all pages, remembered across pages and sessions. Each view keeps its own zoom; the first time, all pages zooms out if the spread would not fit.
  • All pages lays the book out as it is read: cover alone, then facing pages, with a blank page before the back cover when the printed book needs one. Right-to-left books are mirrored, calendars stack, screen-sized books show single pages, and purple corner marks show the page being edited. The other pages are read-only, sandboxed, drawn only near the screen, and follow Format dialog changes. None of it is saved.
  • Clicking another page opens it in the same place on screen and replays the click (caret in text, picture selected).
  • Page changes: with one page, the new page shows in place as soon as it is drawn. With all pages, the corner marks move to the clicked page at once and the old page stays until the new one and its neighbours are drawn. This relies on BL-13502's saving without a reload, now on master.
  • No input until the page is ready: until every CKEditor has started and the page's delayed setup is done, the page and the toolbox show a wait cursor and take no clicks or keys, because CKEditor, as it finishes starting, wipes out anything typed since. At five times slower CPU that is about 3 s per change with one page and 5 to 8 s with all pages.
  • Remove Page marks the page being edited with a red X while the confirmation is up, and the controls above the page sit in a bar fixed across the top of the page frame.
