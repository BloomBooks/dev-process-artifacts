What this PR is about

Problem

Books migrated from Little Zebra keep their page margins when the user switches to the Edge to Edge theme. On A4/A5/A6 pages, pictures and "Size to fit image" never reach the page edge, and Bloom gives no warning. Deleting the book's customBookStyles2.css makes it work.

Cause

Little Zebra's migrated customBookStyles2.css sets the per-side margin variables directly on .A4Portrait, .A5Portrait and so on. Edge to Edge only set the general --page-margin, which the per-side variables normally fall back to, so the custom values won. This appeared in 6.6 because BL-15958 widened Edge to Edge from the Ebook/Device sizes, which Little Zebra's CSS never targets, to every page size.

What the PR does

  • Numbered pages: Edge to Edge's numbered-page rule now also sets the four per-side margins to var(--page-margin) (0). That two-class rule beats the custom CSS's one-class page-size rules.
  • Front and back matter: a div.bloom-frontMatter, div.bloom-backMatter rule sets the per-side margins, and the outside covers' top and bottom margins, from the theme's own margin: 3mm, or the standard margin with Full Bleed. The div is just enough to beat Little Zebra's one-class rules. Two-class rules, which xMatter packs use for their own covers and title pages, still win, as do custom layouts and image covers.
  • customBookStyles2.css stays linked, so its other styles still apply, and the EFL migrations that pair it with a non-default theme are unaffected.
  • Unit tests check that the declarations sit in those two rules.

Checked in a running Bloom on a copy of a Little Zebra book at A5 with Edge to Edge:

  • Numbered pages: 12mm before, 0mm now.
  • Inside front cover, title page and credits (no Full Bleed): now 3mm.
  • Outside front cover: 3mm at the sides. The top and bottom are still Little Zebra's 10mm (the decision below).

Worth knowing

  • Under Edge to Edge, a two-class xMatter rule that sets margins on every page now gives way to the theme on numbered pages. ABC-Reader's .HalfLetterPortrait.bloom-page is one, so its content pages lose their 0.5in margins. That is what Edge to Edge is for, but it is a change for such books.
  • Not driven by hand: PDF and BloomPUB output. The running-Bloom check measured each page's margins in Edit.

1. Little Zebra margins at the top and bottom of portrait covers

Your instruction was to beat rules like .A5Portrait.outsideFrontCover. I did not. The theme loads before customBookStyles2.css, so beating those two-class rules takes a rule of three classes, or of two classes plus an element. That rule would also beat the same-strength rules the Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan xMatter packs use for their own cover margins (0mm and the bleed width). Those covers would get 3mm under Edge to Edge.

So in A4, A5 and A6 Portrait, a Little Zebra book's outside front and back covers keep Little Zebra's top and bottom margins (10–15mm) under Edge to Edge. Their sides, and every other front/back-matter page, now follow the theme. The test-ideas comment already tells testers this is expected.
