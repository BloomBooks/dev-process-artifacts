What this PR is about

Problem

Books migrated from Little Zebra keep their page margins when the user switches to the Edge to Edge theme. On A4/A5/A6 pages, pictures and "Size to fit image" never reach the page edge, and Bloom gives no warning. Deleting the book's customBookStyles2.css makes it work.

Cause

Little Zebra's migrated customBookStyles2.css sets the per-side margin variables directly on .A4Portrait, .A5Portrait and so on. Edge to Edge only set the general --page-margin: 0mm, which the per-side variables normally fall back to, so the custom 12mm values won. This appeared in 6.6 because BL-15958 widened Edge to Edge from the Ebook/Device sizes, which Little Zebra's CSS never targets, to every page size.

What the PR does

  • Edge to Edge's numbered-page rule now also sets the four per-side margins to var(--page-margin) (0). Content pages then have no margins under Edge to Edge, whatever custom CSS sets them per page size.
  • customBookStyles2.css stays linked, so its other styles still apply, and the EFL migrations that pair it with a non-default theme are unaffected.
  • A unit test checks that the four declarations sit inside that numbered-page rule.

Front and back matter are left alone. An override for them was tried and reverted, because the Slack discussion favours fixing Little Zebra books in the migration instead: a Little Zebra theme in place of customBookStyles2.css.

Checked in a running Bloom on a copy of a Little Zebra book at A5 with Edge to Edge and Full Bleed. Numbered pages went from 12mm per-side margins to 0mm, with customBookStyles2.css still linked.

Worth knowing

  • Until the migration work lands, a Little Zebra book under Edge to Edge has no margins on content pages but keeps Little Zebra's margins on its covers, title page and credits. The card's test ideas tell testers not to report that.
  • Under Edge to Edge, an xMatter rule that sets margins on every page, such as ABC-Reader's .HalfLetterPortrait.bloom-page, now gives way on numbered pages.

1. Where the Little Zebra theme migration goes

The Slack plan is to ship a Little Zebra theme built from the two migration files and migrate Little Zebra books to it instead of writing customBookStyles2.css. It also needs a one-time conversion of books that are already migrated, or the books this card was reported on stay as they are. Hatton handed the design to you and John Thomson. Tracking it on its own card lets this PR merge for 6.6 while that design settles.
