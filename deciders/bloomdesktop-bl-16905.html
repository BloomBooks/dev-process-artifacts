What this PR is about

Problem

The two Sample Shells Bloom ships, The Moon and the Cap and A Family Learns about Immunisations, were ten migrations behind (maintenance level 4; current is 14). They had also never been through the 6.5 per-page browser fix-up. So every user who opened one, or made a book from one, had to update it first. Opening the AI image editor or changing the page size then brought up the “Bloom needs to update the pages of this book…” dialog.

What the PR does

  • Runs Update Book (the whole-book migrations plus the off-screen per-page fix-up) on both shells, so they ship at the current levels, stamped for A5 Portrait, with the new back-cover “Made with Bloom” badge and language QR images they reference. The old tagline image is removed. Each shell also ships the Default branding’s branding.css, which sizes that badge; without it, previewing the shell showed the QR code at its full 740px. A copy of a freshly made English collection produced the same books apart from its country.
  • Makes SampleShells.BloomCollection English/English with no sign language and Traditional front/back matter. Whatever that collection says leaks into the books on update: its old Tok Pisin second language added empty Tok Pisin text boxes throughout, and a missing sign language made Bloom assume English.
  • Removes the old lowercase data-l1/l2/l3, which Bloom kept beside its own data-L1/L2/L3. The .htm and meta.json stay prettier-formatted like the originals, and git diff -w hides the formatting part. What remains is real Bloom output: the canvas/image migrations, regenerated front/back matter, reordered language boxes and editing-time attributes.
  • Adds ShippedSampleShellsTests, which fails when a maintenance level is bumped and the shells fall behind, or their layout stamp doesn’t match their page size. It points to a short README.md in the Sample Shells folder on how to re-update them.
