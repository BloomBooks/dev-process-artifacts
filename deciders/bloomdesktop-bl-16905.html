What this PR is about

Problem

The two Sample Shells Bloom ships, The Moon and the Cap and A Family Learns about Immunisations, were ten migrations behind (maintenance level 4; current is 14). They had also never been through the 6.5 per-page browser fix-up. So every user who opened one, or made a book from one, had to update it first. Opening the AI image editor or changing the page size then brought up the “Bloom needs to update the pages of this book…” dialog.

What the PR does

  • Runs Update Book (the whole-book migrations plus the off-screen per-page fix-up) on both shells. They now ship at the current levels, stamped as processed for A5 Portrait, with the new back-cover “Made with Bloom” badge and language QR images they reference. The old tagline image they no longer use is removed.
  • Records an empty sign language in SampleShells.BloomCollection. Without it, Bloom assumes English, and that leaked into the books (“English, English” as the book’s languages).
  • Adds ShippedSampleShellsTests. It fails when a maintenance level is bumped and the shells fall behind, or when their layout stamp doesn’t match their page size, and its message says how to re-update them.
