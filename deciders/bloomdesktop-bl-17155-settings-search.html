What this PR is about

Purpose

Collection Settings has grown to many pages, and people can't easily find the setting they want. This PR turns on the settings library's (config-r's) search, so a person types a word and sees every matching setting from every page at once, found by its name, its explanation, its page or group heading, or one of its drop-down choices.

What the PR does

  • Moves Bloom to config-r 1.0.0-alpha.38, which has search and lets Bloom set the match color from the theme, the back arrow's color, and a small search box.
  • Puts the search box in the title bar of Collection Settings and of Book and Page Settings, through one shared hook. Matches are highlighted in yellow.
  • Names the written language in a group heading whenever the row that shows the language is out of sight: "Written Language 1 · English" in search results and on a More page's back button. Searching for a language's name finds its settings.
  • Stops a dialog's title bar from starting a drag when the press is on a control in it, so pressing in the search box selects text instead of moving the dialog.
  • Gives every config-r settings form one type scale and one subscription badge that lines up with the control above it, puts the restart message beside the Restart button, and makes the subscription code's Copy and Paste buttons smaller.
