Decision · your rename vs. John's suggestion

The new name answers half of what John asked for

John's last comment was about the risk of a future reader picking the wrong method. He wrote that he still did not like

"the different names which seem to mean the same, but are different enough to leave the reader wondering what the difference is…and as you say, there's an important difference, so we don't want a new user picking the wrong one."

His reading of the method was that its real job is to be where the shell finishes loading — the flag being the critical part, with coming to the front merely one of the things worth doing at that moment. On that reading he proposed FinishLoading() or OnFinishedLoading().

You went to FinishPuttingShellInFront, which centres the coming-to-front instead. That is defensible on a point John's suggestion misses: the method's third caller is the static Shell.ComeToFront(), which serves the external/bringToFront endpoint that bloomlibrary.org calls to surface Bloom, and one more path after a login. Those fire long after loading has finished, where a method called OnFinishedLoading() would read as plainly wrong. So his name would have been accurate at two of three call sites and misleading at the third; yours is accurate at all three.

What it does not do is separate the two methods on the axis John was worried about. A reader still meets BringToFrontNow and FinishPuttingShellInFront — both named after putting the window in front — and the dangerous difference between them, that only one of them also declares loading finished, is still invisible in the name. It is spelled out in the doc comment directly above, which is the mitigation, and John approved either way.

Mechanical: the same three call sites and one comment, ~5 lines, no behavior change. It closes John's concern squarely, since the name is then unmistakably not just another bring-to-front — at the cost of being long, and of a second rename commit on an already-approved PR.
Same mechanical cost, but I would not recommend it: it reads wrongly at the external/bringToFront call site, which is the objection worth putting to John rather than absorbing.

Recommended because John explicitly released this — "if it doesn't seem worth changing (or not even desirable) you can just merge" — the approval is already in hand, and your name is the more accurate of the two proposals. Renaming again means another commit and another gauntlet on a PR that is otherwise ready to go.
