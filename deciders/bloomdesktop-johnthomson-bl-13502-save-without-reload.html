What this PR is about

A follow-up to #8209 (saving a page without reloading it), from testing of the game pages' Play tab.

Problem

On a game page, a save made while the tester had items dragged around in Play could write the tester's positions into the book over the author's. Clicking the Play tab while it was already selected did it reliably. A save made mid-drag, or while Play was in use with another tool open, could also store play-only markup in the book.

Cause

Bloom Player's prepareActivity() records where each draggable is as the author's position, and nothing stopped Bloom calling it again while the page was already in Play. The save only cleaned Play mode off its copy when the Game tool was open, though the Play tab works with any tool. And undoPrepareActivity() left some play markup behind.

What the PR does

  • A page is prepared for Play only once per time it enters Play.
  • Play mode belongs to the game page's tabs, not the Game tool: every save cleans it off its copy whatever tool is open, using bloom-player's undoPrepareActivity(), which #446 makes remove everything playing adds.
  • Play is for trying the game, not editing it: the toolbox and the top bar's editing controls are disabled and dimmed, editing keys and paste are blocked, and every text box on the page is locked, until Start.
  • Play and image descriptions never overlap: Play is disabled while they show, and showing them leaves Play.
  • Playing a page no longer makes it look edited afterwards (text is locked with contenteditable="false" rather than by removing the attribute).
