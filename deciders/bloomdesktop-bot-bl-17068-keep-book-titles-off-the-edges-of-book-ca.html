What this PR is about

Problem

In Publish ▸ Apps ▸ Choose Books, a book name long enough to fill a line ran right up to the left and right edges of its dark card (reported by Colin on 6.6 alpha).

What the PR does

  • Adds 6px of padding on each side of the card's title (BookLinkCard.tsx:122), so a name that would have touched the edges wraps onto its second line instead.
  • The two-line limit, the "…" and the card's height are unchanged.
  • The same card appears in the Book Grid setup dialog and the book list for making a link to another book; they get the same margin.
