What this PR is about

Problem

In an Edge to Edge book with full bleed on, a text box's format gear (the cog at its bottom left) sits about 20px in from the corner instead of at the corner when Page Numbers is Automatic or Left. Colin reported it on BL-15958 ("Gear Icon Placement").

Cause

Edge to Edge moves the gear 20px right on every numbered page. Without full bleed that is needed: the text box reaches the bottom of the page and the page number sits over its bottom-left corner. With full bleed, PR #8462 keeps text the standard page margin from the cut line, so the page number sits in that margin below the text box and there is nothing to move out of the way of.

What the PR does

  • Under full bleed, the theme sets both of its gear-offset variables to 0 on numbered pages (appearance-theme-edge-to-edge.css:160). Books without full bleed are unchanged.
  • A new e2e test, edge-to-edge-format-gear.spec.ts (Notion Test Case ID 857), checks where the gear sits for each Page Numbers setting, with and without full bleed.
