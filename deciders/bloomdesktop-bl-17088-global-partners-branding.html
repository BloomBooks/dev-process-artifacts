What this PR is about

Purpose

Global Partners has subscribed to Bloom Enterprise and needs a branding pack for its two descriptors, Global-Partners and Global-Partners[vanilla]. New books should carry their copyright, license notes, back-cover text and back-cover logo as defaults the user can change; the vanilla variant should carry only the Made with Bloom badge.

What the PR does

  • Adds presets for the back cover (their logo beside the plain Made with Bloom badge), a default "Copyright © <year> Global Partners", their "Translations outside of Global Partners" and "Adaptations" license notes, and their back-cover sentence. No title-page logo, as they asked.
  • License notes go only on genuinely new books (as GRN-REACH does), not on translations of another publisher's shell. Their opening "You are free to use…" paragraph is covered by Bloom's standard CC BY text.
  • The vanilla variant puts only the badge on the back cover.
  • The stylesheet sets the logo and badge in a row, puts a blank line above the license notes, and shrinks the notes to 8pt on the three smallest page sizes, where the credits page overflowed.
