What this PR is about

Problem

In a settings row, the label and its control didn't line up. Checkboxes sat about 12px below their label's text, and dropdowns and text boxes sat 6-7px low.

Cause

ConfigrRowTwoColumns lined up the top of the label with the top of the control. A control's text or box rarely starts at its top: a checkbox has 9px of padding around its box, and dropdowns and text boxes have padding above their text.

What the PR does

  • A row now lines up its label with the first line of text in its control (CSS baseline alignment). Controls with text line up whatever their height or padding, and a multi-line text box keeps its label beside its first line.
  • A control with no text has no baseline. A row with the new alignControlLikeTextField prop gives it an invisible line of text where a one-line outlined text field puts its text: across the middle of a control no taller than a text field (checkbox, color swatch, arrow), and at a text field's depth in a taller one, so a tall control's label sits near its top, not its middle. ConfigrBoolean and ConfigrPage set the prop themselves; the ConfigrCustom*Input components accept it, so an app sets it on any custom control that shows no text (Bloom has three).
  • The required-field asterisk loses its old hand positioning; one-column rows also align on the baseline, so it sits on the text line there too.

Measured in Bloom, every row in Book Settings, Collection Settings and App Builder Settings now lines up with its label within about 1.5px, down from 4-20px, and every checkbox exactly.
