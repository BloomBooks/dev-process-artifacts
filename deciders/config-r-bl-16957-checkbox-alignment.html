What this PR is about

Problem

In a settings row, the label and its control didn't line up. Checkboxes sat about 12px below their label's text, and dropdowns and text boxes sat 6-7px low.

Cause

ConfigrRowTwoColumns lined up the top of the label with the top of the control. A control's text or box rarely starts at its top: a checkbox has 9px of padding round its box, and dropdowns and text boxes have padding above their text.

What the PR does

  • A row now lines up its label and its control on their first line of text (CSS baseline alignment). Controls with text line up whatever their height or padding, and a multi-line text box keeps its label by its first line.
  • A control with no text has no baseline, so the new exported ConfigrCenterOnTextLine gives it an invisible line of text and centres it on that line. config-r uses it for its checkbox and switch, the page arrow and ConfigrColorPicker; an app wraps any custom control of its own that shows no text (Bloom has three).
  • The required-field asterisk loses the hand positioning it needed under top alignment.

Measured in Bloom, every row in Book Settings, Collection Settings and App Builder Settings is now within about 1.5px of its label, down from 4-20px.
