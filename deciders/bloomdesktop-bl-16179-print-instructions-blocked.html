What this PR is about

Problem

A booklet only comes out right with particular print settings (landscape, flip on short edge, the paper size, actual size). On PDF & Print, Bloom listed them in a blue banner along the bottom of the screen when you clicked Print..., but the print dialog that opens at the same moment covers the whole screen, banner included. Nobody could read the settings while choosing them, and the dialog can't be moved out of the way.

Cause

The print dialog is the browser's own print preview. It covers everything in the WebView2 that asked to print, and nothing drawn inside that page can appear above it.

What the PR does

  • In the two booklet modes, Print... now opens the instructions in a small window of their own, owned by Bloom's main window, before the print dialog. It sits at the top right of Bloom, clear of the dialog's settings and its Print and Cancel buttons; it never takes the keyboard, stays with Bloom when you switch programs, can be dragged, and has a normal close X. Its title bar is blank, and screen readers get its heading as its name.
  • WebView2 gives no sign when the print dialog closes, so the window stays until the user closes it, prints again, changes the booklet mode, or leaves PDF & Print.
  • The window has one of two fixed heights, with or without a note, that fit the text in every language we ship; a longer future translation scrolls. Its background is the page's blue, so it doesn't flash white. When Bloom is narrower than the window, its left edge stays on the screen.
  • The old banner is gone. Same localized strings as before; no new strings.
  • A new e2e spec, with a helper that reaches the print dialog over Windows UI Automation, checks the window's content, that it fits without scrolling, and when it opens and closes. The helper hands the foreground back after cancelling the dialog, so a run can't take the keyboard from whoever is typing.
