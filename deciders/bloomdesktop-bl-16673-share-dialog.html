What this PR is about

Problem

Bloom already lets a team work on a collection together through Team Collections, but only by having an administrator share a folder with a file-sync service such as Dropbox. Bloom has no control over that sharing: who can reach the folder, and what they may do there, is up to the sync service and the administrator's skill with it. Cloud Team Collections (BL-16531) keep the same way of working, but put the shared copy on a backend Bloom controls (Supabase), which we hope will also be more reliable. That control is what makes it possible for Bloom to have its own UI for sharing: administrators invite people from inside Bloom and decide what each may do. This PR adds that UI, which will eventually replace the Team Collection settings panel. The backend ships separately, from bloom-core-supabase, and isn't deployed yet.

What the PR does

  • A new Share button in the Collection tab's top bar opens a Share dialog built from the card's mockups.
  • Admins invite people by email as Admin or Editor, change roles from a menu that describes each role, and remove people. Nobody can change their own role or remove themselves, so an admin steps down only once someone else has taken over, and a collection always keeps an admin. Editors see the list read-only. A Learn about sharing link opens the help page.
  • People are identified by their BloomLibrary.org sign-in, the identity the cloud backend will authenticate; a signed-out user is asked to sign in first.
  • The first invitation shares the collection. In a folder Team Collection only its administrators can do that, and the people its history shows are offered as suggestions to invite.
  • SharingApi talks to an ICollectionSharingService. Its only implementation, a stand-in, keeps the record in sharing.local.json in the collection folder. It enforces the backend's rules: all-or-nothing invitations, safe writes. Invitations aren't sent anywhere yet.
  • TopBarButton/BloomButton can take an icon element (the MUI Share icon) instead of an image file.
