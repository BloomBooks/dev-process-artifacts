What this PR is about

Problem

Bloom has no way for someone to share a collection with other people. Sharing is moving to a cloud backend (Supabase, from the BL-16531 work), and that needs a UI where a collection's administrators decide who can work on it and in what role. It will eventually replace the Team Collection settings panel. The backend ships separately from bloom-core-supabase and isn't deployed yet.

What the PR does

  • A new Share button in the Collection tab's top bar opens a Share dialog built from the card's mockups.
  • Admins invite people by email as Admin or Editor, change roles from a menu that describes each role, and remove people. The last admin can be neither demoted nor removed. Editors see the list read-only.
  • People are identified by their BloomLibrary.org sign-in, the identity the cloud backend will authenticate; a signed-out user is asked to sign in first.
  • The first invitation shares the collection. In a folder Team Collection only its administrators can do that, and the people its history shows are offered as suggestions to invite.
  • SharingApi talks to an ICollectionSharingService. Its only implementation, a stand-in, keeps the record in sharing.local.json in the collection folder. It enforces the backend's rules: all-or-nothing invitations, safe writes. Invitations aren't sent anywhere yet.
  • TopBarButton/BloomButton can take an icon element (the MUI Share icon) instead of an image file.

Worth knowing

  • The Team Collection suggestions panel was checked with fake data and unit tests only. It hasn't been clicked through in a real Team Collection with history.
  • Testing the dialog for real writes sharing.local.json into the collection's own folder. Delete it to reset a collection to "not shared". A whole-collection archive carries it along (answered on the PR; it's a stand-in).
  • Nothing links to "Learn about sharing" yet, and the enterprise-subscription badge mentioned on the card isn't included.

An admin can hand admin rights to someone who has never signed in

Right now an admin may demote or remove themselves as long as another Admin remains, even if that other Admin is only invited and has never signed in. With the real backend that can leave a collection whose only admin has never actually joined, so nobody can manage sharing until they do. The card's comments ask whether changing your own role should be allowed at all; it currently is, with the last-admin rule as the only guard.

Source: card comment on BL-16673 ("it should not be possible to remove the last admin… should it be possible to change your own status?") and the self-demotion check in this run.

"Learn about sharing" link

The mockups put a "Learn about sharing" link at the top right of the dialog. I left it out because there's no help page for it yet, and I didn't want to invent a URL. Adding it is trivial once there's somewhere to point.
