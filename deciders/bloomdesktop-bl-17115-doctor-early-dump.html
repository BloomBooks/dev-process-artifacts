What this PR is about

Looking into BL-17092, a real minute-long freeze that the Freeze Doctor filed with no crash dump, turned up three problems in the Doctor.

1. A freeze ended before 60 s is filed with no dump

Problem. The Doctor dumps Bloom only when it reports a freeze, at 60 s. People end Bloom sooner than that, and then there is nothing left to dump, so nobody can tell what Bloom was stuck in.

What the PR does. At 20 s without a response (Suspect), the Doctor dumps Bloom at once and keeps the file. A later report still prefers a fresh dump. If it can't take one (Bloom has gone, or its runtime doesn't answer within 20 s), it attaches the early dump as bloom-<pid>-early.dmp and says when it was taken. If Bloom recovers, the early dump is thrown away. The early dumps of the last few Blooms are kept, so restarting Bloom straight away doesn't lose the one just killed. Each freeze gets one early dump, and deletes happen off the watcher's timer. The Doctor also now opens dump files itself, because ClrMD left a dump it failed to read locked open.

2. One freeze was posted as "This happened again"

Problem. A Bloom that froze and then died produced two reports. When the second couldn't be merged into the first, it was posted as a recurrence.

What the PR does. A same-Bloom follow-on now reads "The same Bloom then had a further problem", and says it isn't a second occurrence.

3. A comment claimed a dump that didn't exist

Problem. Recurrence comments always said the user's folder held "the crash dump".

What the PR does. The comment now checks the report's own files, and says "No crash dump was taken for this report" when there is none.
