What this PR is about

The card reported that resizing an inline image behaves differently in portrait and landscape books. Fixing it, and trying it on the same page, turned up two more problems with moving a picture inside its text box.

Resizing stopped short in portrait and went past the page in landscape

Problem. Enlarging a picture by its corner stopped a little short of the edge of the text in a portrait book, and could stretch it past the bottom of the page in a landscape book.

Cause. A resize was limited only by width, at most 95% of the text's width. A wide, short block lets the picture grow taller than the block long before it gets that wide.

Fix. A picture can now be as wide as the text, and a resize stops at the largest width that pushes no text out of the block (continueResize). The gap beside a left or right picture shrinks as the picture nears the full width, so it does not reach past the side of the block (inlineImages.less).

A picture dragged to the very bottom jumped back up

Problem. In a block with room to spare below the text, a picture dragged to the bottom usually jumped back up to just below the text.

Cause. Low in the block the drag chose the bottom dock, which puts the picture right after the last line of text. Separately, the drag did not count the 8px space below a side picture, so the move went 8px past the block and was undone.

Fix. A drag measures once whether the bottom dock would reach the bottom of the block. When it would not, a position low in the block or below it means the left, middle or right dock, by where the pointer is, and those take the picture to the very bottom (computeInlineImageDock). The drag counts a side picture's bottom margin.

A move could push another language's text out

Problem. In a block showing two languages, a drag or resize that pushed the second language's text out was not undone.

Cause. The languages share the block's height and each shrinks in proportion to its text, so the picture's own language hid almost nothing, and that was all the drag checked.

Fix. Drag, resize and the offset fitting after a page size change all measure how much text the block's languages hide together (measureBlockOverflowLayoutPx).
