What this PR is about

Problem

In apps built on Publish > Apps (Reading App Builder), text in Andika shows in the phone's own font, with a two-story "a" instead of Andika's single-story one. Other fonts work. A tester reported it on 6.5.101 Beta.

Cause

BloomPUBs never embed Andika, because Bloom Reader and other hosts supply it to bloom-player. A RAB app doesn't:

  • It ships Andika under names bloom-player never asks for (app-builders #2510).
  • RAB apps have no internet permission, so bloom-player's fallback (downloading Andika from bloomlibrary.org) fails even online.

What the PR does

A stopgap until RAB fixes #2510. It goes to master, and to Version6.5 in a separate PR after it merges.

  • BloomPUBs made for an app now embed Andika like any other font, with only the faces the book uses. Other BloomPUBs are unchanged.
  • Text in the older "Andika New Basic" gets the matching Andika faces, and in an app's BloomPUB the book's references to it are rewritten to Andika.
  • That rewrite, which Bloom already uses for fonts it can't embed, now also catches quoted font names. That part applies to every ePUB and BloomPUB.
  • It fixes only apps built through Bloom. A BloomPUB added to RAB by hand still lacks Andika.
