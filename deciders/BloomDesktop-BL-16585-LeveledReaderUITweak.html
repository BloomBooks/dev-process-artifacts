Decision 1

The 120px floor on the scrolling region

The Leveled Reader's statistics now live in a box that scrolls on its own. In a column like that, something has to be allowed to shrink when space runs short, and this box is the only candidate — the level indicator above it and the button block below it both hold their size. Left unbounded it shrinks all the way to nothing, which is exactly what happened in the first draft: on a short panel the statistics vanished with no scrollbar and no way to get them back.

So the box has a floor of 120 pixels — roughly a section heading plus four rows of figures. Below that the panel gives up on pinning and scrolls as a whole, so nothing is ever unreachable. Devin flagged 120 as a magic number that a larger UI font or a longer translated heading could outgrow. That is true, and it is also how the Decodable Reader has always worked: its two sections use hard-coded floors of 100px and 200px, so 120 sits between them and the two tools stay consistent. Being a little off in either direction is harmless — too small shows less before the panel scrolls, too large starts the panel scrolling sooner — which is why leaving it is the recommendation, and why I already replied and resolved the thread on that basis.

Decision 2

A second test-ideas comment rather than a rewrite
