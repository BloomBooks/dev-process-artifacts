What this PR is about

Problem

The PRs below this one let a text block hold a picture that the text flows around, the way a picture sits in a Word document. On their own they give a person no way to add one, put it where they want it, size it, or work on the picture. This PR is that part: everything a person does to a picture in a text block.

What the PR does

  • Insert Image on a text block's right-click menu puts a placeholder at the right of the block, in every language. It is not offered in text boxes on a canvas, or in fields Bloom stores for itself, such as the book title.
  • Dragging docks the picture left, right, in a band across the middle, or at the bottom, or moves it down through the lines; corner handles resize it. Both work at any zoom.
  • The picture's own menu and toolbar are the canvas image menu, with Transparency, Flip and Reset Image. Rotate, Duplicate and the background-image commands are left out. Every command takes an undo point and copies its result to the other languages' copies.
  • Ctrl+Z takes back picture changes through an undo layer for inline images, because CKEditor's own undo restores one language only. Typing keeps its Ctrl+Z.
  • Saving strips the selection and the resize handles, so they never reach the book. The edit-time styling is in its own file, inlineImageEditing.less.
