What this PR is about

Problem

In an Edge to Edge book, full-page pictures should run to every edge of the page. On A4, A3 and Letter pages (portrait or landscape) they stopped short of the inside edge, leaving a white strip by the spine: on the right of left-hand pages, on the left of right-hand pages. A5 books were fine.

Cause

The Default theme gives the larger page sizes (A3, A4, B5, Letter, Legal) a 10mm gutter of extra padding on the inside edge. Edge to Edge zeroed every margin of a content page but never cleared the gutter. A5 and smaller sizes have no gutter.

What the PR does

  • Edge to Edge sets the gutter to 0mm on its content pages, in the rule that lets a gutter chosen in Book Settings still win. Front/back matter and interactive pages are unchanged.
  • The full-bleed margins e2e spec now switches its book to A4 and checks a left and a right page: the picture runs into the bleed on both sides, and the text stays 15mm from the cut.
  • The e2e page-size helper now waits for the Edit tab to settle, so a test can safely change the page right after.
