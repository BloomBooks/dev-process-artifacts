What testing found

Two real defects, neither of which any test or review had caught.

  • The panel didn't refresh itself. Pausing checkouts changed the setting in memory, but the checkout button stayed live until a click forced a refetch. I had claimed InvokeSelectionChanged would refresh it; nothing on that path sends bookTeamCollectionStatus. Fixed by sending it explicitly, via a new SendBookStatusReload alongside the existing SendBookContentReload.
  • The administrator could silently un-pause the whole team. Their own repo watcher is suppressed while Bloom pushes their edit up, so the machine that made the change never acted on it — and its stale in-memory value would be written back over the pause by the next save. Fixed by re-reading the setting straight after the push.
  • Both now verified end to end in a real Team Collection: pausing and un-pausing each took effect in about 19 seconds with no restart and no click, confirmed in the UI and by polling teamCollection/selectedBookStatus.
