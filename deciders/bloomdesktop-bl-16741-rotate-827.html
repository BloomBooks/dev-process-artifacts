What this PR is about

Problem

A page's background picture showed a thin white line along an edge of the picture area again as soon as the author used Rotate right on it. The e2e test for Test Case ID 827 has failed on master since BL-17009 merged: after four rotations the background's left was 0, not the -1 it started at.

Cause

BL-17009 (PR 8459) stops the white line by making a background picture overhang each edge of the picture area it reaches by kBackgroundImageBleedPx (1px). That is the -1. Rotate right lays the rotated picture out itself (computeRotatedBackgroundLayout) and fitted it to the picture area exactly, with no overhang. The -1 is right, and the rotation was dropping it.

What the PR does

  • A rotated background gets the same overhang adjustBackgroundImageSizeToFit gives it: a fitted picture is enlarged by getBackgroundImageSizeWithBleed, and a picture set to fill the page covers the picture area plus 1px on every side. An ordinary picture element that Rotate right turns keeps its own size.
  • The rotation works from exact sizes, not the whole pixels clientWidth and clientHeight round to: the picture area through getExactClientSize, the element and the picture's box from the px sizes Bloom wrote. Whole pixels left as little as half a pixel of overhang on a fractional-width origami pane, and the rounding grew with each rotation once the overhang was added.
  • New and updated unit tests in imageContentTransformSpec.ts, with fixtures that start as Bloom places a background and round sizes as a browser does.

PR 8463, on the same card, also edits rotate-and-flip-images.spec.ts; this PR does not touch that file.
