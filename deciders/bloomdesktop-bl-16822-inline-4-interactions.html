What this PR is about

Problem

The PRs below this one let a text block hold a picture that the text flows around, as in Word. On their own they give a person no way to add one, place it, size it, or work on it. This PR is everything a person does to a picture in a text block.

What the PR does

  • Insert Image on a text block's right-click menu puts a placeholder in every language. It is not offered in canvas text boxes or in fields Bloom stores for itself, such as the book title.
  • Dragging docks the picture left, right, in a middle band or at the bottom, or moves it down through the lines; corner handles resize it. Both work at any zoom. The other pictures stay where they were.
  • The picture's menu and toolbar are the canvas image menu without Rotate, Duplicate and the background-image commands. Flip and Reset Image act on the inline picture itself.
  • Undo and redo: every picture change is one entry on the shared undo stack, made after it has been copied to every language. Ctrl+Z goes to the stack when its next undo is a picture change, so all languages go back together. Ctrl+Y redoes. CKEditor's snapshots are kept current, so it never treats a picture change as typing.
  • Ctrl+A then Delete no longer removes a picture by accident, including one inserted after the page was opened; a deliberate Delete stays deleted.
  • Saving strips the selection class and handles, so they never reach the book.
