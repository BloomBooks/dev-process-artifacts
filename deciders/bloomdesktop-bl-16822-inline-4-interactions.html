What this PR is about

Problem

The PRs below this one let a text block hold a picture that the text flows around, as in Word. On their own they give a person no way to add one, place it, size it, or work on it. This PR is everything a person does to a picture in a text block.

What the PR does

  • Insert Image on a text block's right-click menu puts a placeholder in every language. It is not offered in canvas text boxes or in fields Bloom stores for itself, such as the book title.
  • Dragging docks the picture left, right, in a middle band or at the bottom, or moves it down through the lines; corner handles resize it. Both work at any zoom, and the other pictures stay where they were.
  • Another page size or layout scales each picture's offset to the new block height, then moves the lowest picture up only if that makes the text fit. Measured in Bloom over 4,764 combinations, that fits every case where some position could.
  • The picture's menu and toolbar are the canvas image menu without Rotate, Duplicate and the background-image commands. Flip and Reset Image act on the inline picture itself.
  • Undo and redo: every picture change is one entry on the shared undo stack, made after it has been copied to every language. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y go to the stack when its next step is a picture change, with the caret in or out of the block, and leave the keys to Change Layout mode, form fields and the reader tools' markup mode.
  • CKEditor's own history starts again after a picture change, so it can never put back one language's old copy of a picture. Selecting a picture is not an edit to it, and its undo of typing deselects a picture it replaced.
  • Ctrl+A then Delete no longer removes a picture by accident; a deliberate Delete stays deleted. Saving strips the selection class and handles.
