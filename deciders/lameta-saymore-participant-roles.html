What this PR is about

Problem

SayMore writes a session's participants as Nɔkɔ Ɛsi (consultant);Wilema Dimo (consultant);. When lameta opened such a session, it added contributors named "Nɔkɔ Ɛsi (consultant)" with the role "participant", beside the real contributions for the same people. They matched no person, so they showed up as missing-person problems and as IMDI actors with no details, and they were saved into the file.

What the PR does

  • When lameta moves legacy participants into contributions, text in parentheses at the end of a name becomes the role, if it is an OLAC role id. Other parentheses, such as "Ann (Jr)", stay part of the name.
  • A participant with a role is added unless that person already has a contribution with that role, so a person listed with two roles keeps both.
  • A participant without a role is added as "participant" unless the person already has a contribution or is listed with a role elsewhere in the list. The order of the list is kept.
