What this PR is about

Problem

BL-17002 raised two things about the Image Chooser: the search box was undersized for the dialog, and the preview of the selected image sat in a wide checkerboard "letterbox" even when the picture had no transparency. More generally the dialog wasted space: results in three fixed columns with gaps, and the image details as centered text under the preview.

What the PR does

BloomDesktop #8460 (target Version6.5) pins a restyled gallery from bloom-image-gallery #22 and adds its strings.

  • Search: the field spans the results column, with a compact square button.
  • Results: rows that fill the width, each image at its true shape; a ring and check mark on the selection. When the first page doesn't fill the grid, the next page loads without scrolling. Art of Reading never repeats its results.
  • Preview: the checkerboard is on the picture itself, so it shows only through transparent parts.
  • Sidebar and details pane: a narrower sidebar; details as label/value rows with a "View on <site>" link, and a prompt when nothing is selected.
  • Strings: Common.Dimensions, Common.FileSize, ImageLibrary.SelectImagePrompt (translate="no"); License, Copyright and Illustrator/Photographer reuse existing strings.
