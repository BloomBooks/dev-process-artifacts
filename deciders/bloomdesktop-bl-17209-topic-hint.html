What this PR is about

Problem

The nightly e2e test "the Choose Topic dialog marks the topic picked, and the cover shows it [Test Case ID 88]" has failed since it was added. Its first step, hovering the front cover's topic box to reveal the "Choose topic" link, timed out because the bubble's tip took the pointer.

Cause

The fault is in the test, not in Bloom. The bubble shows whenever the mouse is over the cover page. The test hovered the topic box to get there, but a new book's topic box is empty, and the tip of its own bubble lies over the middle of it, so Playwright's hover at the centre never got through. A person is not affected: the box can't be typed in, and the bubble is the way to the dialog.

What the PR does

The test now hovers the front cover page, which is what shows the bubble, and checks everything it checked before. Before the fix it failed 2 runs out of 2 at the nightly's window size. After it, it passed 13 runs out of 13 at that size and once at the default size.
