What this PR is about

Problem

Publish > Apps (the Reading App Builder screen) would happily build a content-licensed book, such as a Super Bible title, into an Android app in a language the copyright holder has not permitted, English in the Super Bible case. Every other publish path (BloomPUB, ePUB, PDF, upload to Bloom Library) stops with a licensing message. This one showed nothing and produced the app.

Cause

The Reading App Builder workflow never consulted the license checker. It exported a BloomPUB for each book straight from the book's publish settings and handed them to Reading App Builder.

What the PR does

  • Before Prepare or Build exports any BloomPUB, every book headed into the app is checked against the languages its BloomPUB would include, using the same license checker the other paths use. Books whose BloomPUB would merely be reused are checked too, and the check runs before the Reading App Builder workspace is touched.
  • A restricted language makes the step fail with the familiar licensing message in the Apps screen log ("Prepare failed: The copyright holder of this book has not licensed it for publishing in English…"). With several problem books, each line names its book.
  • Four unit tests cover a forbidden language, a permitted language, an unrestricted book, and several problem books, reusing the offline license fixture from the existing LicenseChecker tests.

Worth knowing

  • The fix has not been exercised by hand in a running Bloom with Reading App Builder; it rests on the unit tests and on reading the two entry points (Prepare and Build) that both pass through the checked export. A tester with a Super Bible book is the real confirmation; the test ideas on the card say what to try.
  • The Prepare and Build buttons stay enabled for a restricted book; the message appears only after the click. The BloomPUB and ePUB screens grey out their button instead. This was chosen deliberately to avoid a license-server lookup for every tracked book on every status refresh, and the test ideas tell testers not to report it.
