What this PR is about

Problem

In the Edit tab's one-page view, the source bubble of a text box near the top of the page has its top cut off: its language tabs disappear under the bar that holds the Change Layout switch. Colin found it in 6.6.1331; 6.6.1330 shows the whole bubble.

Cause

BL-16929's All Pages work (PR 8415) put the controls above the page into a bar fixed across the top of the page frame, opaque and at z-index 1000, covering the 45px band Bloom leaves above the page. The band itself did not change; it used to be empty, and bubbles could reach into it. qTip places a source bubble about 27px (before zoom) above its box so its arrow points at the box's top, and the bubbles live in #page-scaling-container, whose zoom transform keeps them under the bar whatever their z-index.

What the PR does

  • When the bar is made, a handler on qTip's tooltipmove event keeps every bubble in the zoom container from starting above the container's top (bloomQtipUtils.ts:77, called from AbovePageControls.tsx:164). With the frame not scrolled, that top is the bar's bottom edge, so the bubble shows in full just below the bar. The limit is in page coordinates, so a bubble still scrolls with its box. Bubbles a dialog puts on the body are left alone.
  • repositionBubbles moved from bookGridView.ts into bloomQtipUtils.ts, so the bar can place again any bubble shown before it existed.

A bubble moved down this way points its arrow up to 27px lower on its box than before.
