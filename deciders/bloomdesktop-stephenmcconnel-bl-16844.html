What this PR is about

Purpose

When Bloom records a talking book as a video or MP3, a narrated page turns the instant its last sentence ends, and people find the result rushed. The only existing timing setting covers pages without narration. This PR adds a pause after the narration before the page turns.

What the PR does

  • Adds a second slider to Publish › Audio or Video, "Pause after narration before turning the page" (0–10 s, default 0, so existing books record as before). The two sliders share one SecondsSlider component, and each is named by its label for screen readers.
  • Saves the value with the book (audioVideo.pauseAfterNarration in publish-settings.json) and passes it to bloom-player as the pauseAfterNarration URL parameter for both the preview and the recording, MP3 included.
  • Fixes the preview's Play button after the preview reloads (a timing slider, the page range, or anything else in its URL), which used to leave a spinner in its place.
  • Updates bloom-player to 2.23.0-alpha.3, which reads the parameter (bloom-player#448).
