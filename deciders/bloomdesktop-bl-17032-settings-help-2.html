What this PR is about

Problem

The new (React) Collection Settings dialog had no Help button. The old WinForms dialog opened a help topic for the tab showing. Config-R, which draws the dialog's pages, could not tell the dialog which page was showing, and could not switch pages while the dialog stayed open.

What the PR does

  • Config-R (sillsdev/config-r#29): ConfigrPane reports the showing page (onTopLevelPageChange), and a ref on it shows another page as clicking its tab does, keeping the user's edits (selectTopLevelPage(pageKey)). initiallySelectedTopLevelPageKey works as before. BL-16734 will use the ref to replace its remount workaround.
  • Bloom (#8489): a Help button at the bottom left opens the topic for the showing page. Languages, Front & Back Matter and Subscription keep the old tabs' topics. The other four pages open the general Settings topic.
  • The Bloom PR needs @sillsdev/config-r 1.0.0-alpha.30, which is published when #29 merges. Until then package.json names alpha.29, and on a clean install Help would always open the general topic.
